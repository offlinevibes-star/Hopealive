About us

A not-for-profit, non-political organisation working for children, youth and communities in Nigeria.

Who we are

HopeAlive Foundation for Education and Community Development works for youth empowerment, education, innovation and community development, with a special commitment to children who are vulnerable or traumatised.

We are based in Aba, Abia State, and registered with Nigeria’s Corporate Affairs Commission as Incorporated Trustees.

Our missionTo protect children, open doors to education and help young people and communities thrive through sustainable, community-led development.

How we are governed

A Board of Trustees leads the Foundation. Trustees are elected at a General Meeting by a two-thirds majority of members present, and the board has between two and fifteen trustees, each serving up to five years.

The Chairman leads the board, the Secretary keeps the common seal, and members meet at an Annual General Meeting.

How we stay accountable

Our constitution builds trust into how we operate.

Registered
Incorporated Trustees, CAC IT No. 8700520, registered on 14 August 2025. Tax ID (TIN): 33447613-0001.
Funds serve our mission
Income and property are applied solely to our objectives. None is paid out as profit to members.
Audited every year
Independent, licensed auditors review our accounts annually, and the audited statements are filed with the CAC.
If we ever dissolve
Remaining assets go to a charitable organisation with similar objectives, never to individual members.
